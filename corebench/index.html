Questions

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs take 1 to 3 days. Parts that must be ordered can add a few days, and we tell you before we start.

Do you fix laptops too?

Yes. Screens, batteries, keyboards, fans and software problems.

Is my data safe?

We never open your personal files. For repairs that risk data, we back up first, with your permission.
